# Word Imposter Privacy Policy (draft)

**Last updated:** [insert date]

Word Imposter is a party game. The initial release is designed to work offline. Player names, custom word packs, and game settings are stored locally on the device. The app does not need an account or a remote database for pass-the-phone gameplay.

The app does not intentionally transmit player names, custom words, or game activity to a server in this offline release. If future versions add analytics, crash reporting, cloud backups, accounts, or online multiplayer, this policy must be updated before those features are released.

You can remove locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Exported word-pack files are managed by the user and may be stored outside the app.
